About Jalaram

A Little Bowl of Surat's Soul

At Jalaram Locho, we believe that great food doesn't need to be complicated. Our locho is prepared fresh, steamed to perfection and served hot with our special toppings and chutneys.

From a quick morning breakfast to an evening snack with friends, every plate is prepared with the same love and authentic Surat street-food flavour.
